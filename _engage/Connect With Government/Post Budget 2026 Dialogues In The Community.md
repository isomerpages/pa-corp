<p>Prime Minister and Minister for Finance, Mr Lawrence Wong will deliver
Singapore’s FY2026 Budget Statement in Parliament on Thursday, 12 February
2026.</p>
<p>Together with MOF and various agencies, People’s Association (PA) has
been engaging the public in the lead-up to Budget 2026. Building on these
conversations, PA and its grassroots organisations will be organising post-Budget
dialogues in the community to engage residents on Budget 2026 measures.&nbsp;</p>
<p>Residents who are interested to attend these post-Budget dialogues to
find out more about Budget 2026 measures can stay tuned to this page. We
will be sharing more details soon!</p>
